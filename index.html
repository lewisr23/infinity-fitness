How a standing order works

A standing order is a repeat payment you set up yourself inside your own banking app. We never hold your card or bank details, and you stay in control - you can change or cancel it any time, without asking us.

Barclays
  1. Log in, then tap Pay & Transfer.
  2. Choose Manage payments, then Standing orders.
  3. Tap Create standing order and enter the details above.
Lloyds & Halifax
  1. Log in and open the account you want to pay from.
  2. Tap Payments & transfers, then Manage standing orders.
  3. Choose Set up a standing order and enter the details above.
NatWest & RBS
  1. Log in and tap Payments.
  2. Choose Manage standing orders, then Create.
  3. Enter the details above and set it to repeat monthly.
Santander
  1. Log in and select your account.
  2. Tap Manage, then Standing orders.
  3. Choose New standing order and enter the details above.
Monzo & Starling
  1. Open Payments, then Scheduled (Monzo) or Outgoing (Starling).
  2. Add a new scheduled payment and pick Monthly.
  3. Enter the details above.
Any other bank
  1. Search your banking app for “standing order” - most apps have it under Payments or Move money.
  2. Set it to repeat monthly, starting on the date you join.
  3. Enter the details above, including the reference.

Menu wording differs between apps and changes with updates. If you get stuck, ask at the desk and we will walk you through it.
